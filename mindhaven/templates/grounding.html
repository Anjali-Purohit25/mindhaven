👁️ The 5-4-3-2-1 Sensory Grounding Technique

Ground your mind back to the physical present moment by locating:

5
Acknowledge 5 things you can SEE around you.

Look for a pattern on a wall, light reflecting on an object, a shadow, or a pencil.

4
Acknowledge 4 things you can physically TOUCH.

Feel your feet flat on the floor, the texture of your shirt, the cool edge of your desk.

3
Acknowledge 3 things you can HEAR.

Listen for distant traffic, the hum of a fan, birds chirping outside, or your own breath.

2
Acknowledge 2 things you can SMELL.

The scent of fresh air, soap, coffee, or rain.

1
Acknowledge 1 thing you can TASTE.

Take a sip of cool water and notice how it feels in your mouth.
